Day [3]: Foundations of Penetration Testing & The Hacker Mindset

📅 Date: September 19, 2026

📝 Summary:
Today was focused on building a strong theoretical foundation in offensive security by understanding the core principles of penetration testing. I explored the fundamental differences between ethical hacking and malicious attacks, diving deep into rules of engagement, scope, and the root causes of why system vulnerabilities exist in the first place.

🛠️ Tools & Concepts Explored:

Penetration Testing Fundamentals: Understood pentesting as a proactive, authorized assessment to identify and remediate weaknesses before adversaries can exploit them.

Scope & Rules of Engagement: Differentiated ethical pentesting (permission-based, defined scope, wide coverage) from malicious hacking (unauthorized, no scope limits, purely target-focused).

Target Focus Areas: Explored Web Application pentesting (testing authentication, authorization, and session cookies) and Network pentesting (External perimeter like firewalls/VPNs vs. Internal lateral movement).

Risk Terminology: Clarified the definitions and relationships between Vulnerabilities, Threats, and Risks.

Root Causes of Vulnerabilities: Learned why flaws exist in the real world, attributing them to human assumptions (e.g., trusting file uploads without validation), software bugs, over-customization, system complexity, and fundamental design flaws.

The Hacker Mindset & Ethics: Emphasized the importance of thinking creatively while maintaining strict ethical boundaries, acquiring proper permission, and building trust with the client.
